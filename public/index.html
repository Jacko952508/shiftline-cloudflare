SETTINGS

Adaptive balances detail and speed. Battery Saver cuts heavy rendering work. The performance display shows actual frame rate on your device.

LOW keeps detailed ships, tunnels, obstacles, shadows and sharp textures, with a themed gradient instead of distant scenery. ADAPTIVE keeps the full world and automatically trims internal resolution by up to 22% when frame rates dip; it restores detail once performance settles.

CONTROLS & WEAPONS

Drag to steer. Tap BOOST to engage; tap again to cancel. When boost empties, tap again to re-engage after charging. Tap a weapon icon to select it, then hold the attack button below BOOST to fire. Release to stop. Pulse shoots straight and cools for 3 seconds after 20 shots. Homing lock brackets identify the targeted rival. Two homing missiles track a visible rival; each charge returns 30 seconds after launch. Obstacles and racers stop missiles; defence cannot break their tracking. One mine recharges 15 seconds after deployment. Q cycles weapons; F fires; Space toggles boost. Automatic shields consume a defence charge when a pulse or mine would hit. Missiles bypass them. Every clean obstacle pass awards 6 boost; near misses award 14 total, larger rings 16 and tight rings 24. Fuel is capped at 100.
